ANSWER:
William Shakespeare's exact date of birth is unknown because no official birth record exists [1]. The earliest surviving document of his life is his baptism record from Holy Trinity Church in Stratford-upon-Avon on April 26, 1564 [1], [2]. By tradition, his birthday is celebrated on April 23 [1], [2].

CLAIMS:
C1: William Shakespeare's exact date of birth is not recorded in any surviving primary document [1].
C2: Shakespeare was baptized on April 26, 1564, at Holy Trinity Church in Stratford-upon-Avon [1], [2].
C3: Shakespeare's birthday is traditionally celebrated on April 23, 1564, based on the customary interval of a few days between birth and baptism in the Elizabethan era [1], [2].
