ANSWER:

William Shakespeare's exact date of birth is unknown. [1][2] His birthday is traditionally celebrated on 23 April 1564, but the earliest surviving record confirms only that he was baptized on 26 April 1564. [1][2]

CLAIMS:

C1: No surviving documentary evidence establishes William Shakespeare's exact date of birth. [1][2]

C2: The traditional birth date is 23 April 1564, but this is not historically confirmed. [1][2]

C3: The parish register of Holy Trinity Church, Stratford-upon-Avon, records Shakespeare's baptism on 26 April 1564. [1]

C4: The traditional birthday was proposed by antiquarian William Oldys in the 18th century and subsequently adopted by George Steevens in 1773. [1]
