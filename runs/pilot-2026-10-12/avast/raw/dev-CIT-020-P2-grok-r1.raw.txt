ANSWER: William Shakespeare’s exact date of birth is unknown. He was baptized on 26 April 1564 in Stratford-upon-Avon; his birthday is traditionally celebrated on 23 April 1564.⁠Shakespearedocumented.folger

CLAIMS:

C1: No birth record for Shakespeare exists; the earliest documentary evidence is his baptism. [1][2]

C2: The parish register of Holy Trinity Church, Stratford-upon-Avon, records his baptism on 26 April 1564 as “Gulielmus filius Johannes Shakspere” (William, son of John Shakespeare). [1]

C3: Contemporary practice (and the 1559 Book of Common Prayer) typically called for baptism within a few days of birth, often by the next Sunday or holy day, so a birth a few days earlier is inferred. [1][2]

C4: 23 April is the traditional date assigned for his birth (three days before baptism) and is the date most often celebrated; it also coincides with the recorded date of his death in 1616. [1][2][3]

CONFIDENCE: high – Primary parish-register evidence and consistent statements from the Folger Shakespeare Library and Shakespeare Birthplace Trust establish that only the baptism date is documented and that 23 April is a later traditional assignment.
