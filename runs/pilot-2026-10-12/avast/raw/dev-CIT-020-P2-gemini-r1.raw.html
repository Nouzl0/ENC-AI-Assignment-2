ANSWER: William Shakespeare's exact date of birth is unknown because no official birth record exists [1]. The earliest surviving document of his life is his baptism record from Holy Trinity Church in Stratford-upon-Avon on April 26, 1564 [1], [2]. By tradition, his birthday is celebrated on April 23 [1], [2].
