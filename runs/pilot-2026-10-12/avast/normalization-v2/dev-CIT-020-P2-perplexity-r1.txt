ANSWER: Shakespeare’s exact date of birth is not known. The traditional and most commonly cited date is 23 April 1564, but it is an inference from his recorded baptism on 26 April 1564, not a documented birth record. [www.shakespeare.org.uk](https://www.shakespeare.org.uk/explore-shakespeare/shakespedia/shakespeares-birthplace/documentary-evidence/) [www.shakespeare.org.uk](https://www.shakespeare.org.uk/explore-shakespeare/shakespedia/william-shakespeare/when-was-shakespeare-born/)

C3: 23 April 1564 is traditionally celebrated as his birthday because baptisms commonly occurred within about three days of birth, making 23 April the earliest plausible birth date.
