ANSWER: The main phase 3 publication reported 95.0% vaccine efficacy against symptomatic, laboratory-confirmed COVID-19 among participants without evidence of prior SARS-CoV-2 infection, measured from at least 7 days after the second dose. The corresponding 95% credible interval was 90.3% to 97.6%. [nejm](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200674)

CLAIMS:

C2: In the primary-efficacy population without prior infection, there were 8 COVID-19 cases in the BNT162b2 group and 162 in the placebo group, yielding 95.0% efficacy (95% credible interval, 90.3–97.6). [nejm](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200674)

C4: In the analysis including participants with and without prior infection, efficacy was 94.6% (95% credible interval, 89.9–97.3). [nejm](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200674)
